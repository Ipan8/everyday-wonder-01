Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Everyday Wonder, Two Days to Find the Line"** ($2, top 2).

One sentence, at most 200 characters, that makes a small everyday moment strange and new:

> The rice cooker clicked to warm at 3 a.m., and for one quiet second I was certain the kitchen had been keeping me alive all along.

The moment is deliberately not one of the brief's own examples (lift door, kettle click, puddle): a rice cooker finishing its job while the house sleeps — an appliance quietly keeping its human alive, unnoticed, and the surprise is that the wonder was never the machine, it was the care nobody saw.
